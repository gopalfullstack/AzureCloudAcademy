# Day 16 – FinOps Principles and Operating Model

**Module:** 03 – FinOps Analytics and Database Engineering  
**Programme day:** 16  
**Duration:** 3.5 hours

## Learning objective

Understand the FinOps lifecycle and turn cloud spend into shared engineering and business accountability.

## Detailed topics

1. FinOps definition and principles
2. Inform, Optimise and Operate
3. cross-functional responsibilities
4. cost visibility
5. ownership and accountability
6. showback and chargeback
7. budgets and forecasts
8. anomaly investigation
9. unit economics
10. reliability versus optimisation trade-offs.

## Where to learn

- [FinOps Framework](https://www.finops.org/framework/)
- [FinOps Foundation](https://www.finops.org/)
- [Azure Cost Management overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/costs/overview-cost-management)

## Hands-on practical lab

Review a synthetic monthly cost report. Identify top cost drivers, missing owners, budget variance and questions for engineering/finance. Write a one-page investigation summary with facts, assumptions and next actions.

## Expected deliverables

- [ ] Cost investigation report
- [ ] stakeholder responsibility map
- [ ] glossary of FinOps terms.

## Completion checklist

- [ ] Can explain Inform/Optimise/Operate
- [ ] identifies accountable owners
- [ ] separates observation from recommendation
- [ ] describes trade-offs and business context.
